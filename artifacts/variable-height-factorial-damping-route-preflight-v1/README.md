# Factorial damping-route preflight v1

This is the result of the transient height-by-velocity preflight frozen in commit
`d514d9e`. It restarted from the preserved source and used the same expanded native
80,454-edge/883-bias mask and fixed 12,314/303 parameter metric as the preceding route
tests. Four matched image histories per scene separated the endpoint throttle response
into transient marker-error, velocity, common and interaction components. The actor
still received only RGB and roll/pitch and used only native recurrence.

The preflight passed at full scale. The damping gradient and height-component Jacobian
were generated by deterministic replays of exactly the same cases; their independently
constructed height components differed by at most `3.73e-8`. Bound-aware projection
froze 696 nonnegative-weight coordinates and converged in three iterations. After edge
bounds, the maximum predicted height-component change was `3.56e-9` of source, far below
the preregistered `1e-3` limit.

The projected damping-loss derivative was -0.00021962. A forward finite difference was
-0.00021124, a 3.82% relative error against the 20% limit. Full-scale replay improved
damping NRMSE by 0.0001865 on the fixed-prefix bank and 0.0001498 on the separately
seeded complete-zero-state bank, both above the `1e-4` floor. This direction was weaker
than the unprotected raw descent, as expected: projection retained about 28% of its
first-order damping slope.

Every matched scene retained its transient marker-error response. Fixed-prefix ratios
ranged from 0.999995 to 1.000000 and complete-zero-state ratios from 0.999546 to
1.000319. The existing small/medium visual-height assay retained 0.99910/0.99926 of
source; RPY, validity and motor-bound checks also passed. Common-throttle RMS changed by
0.000383, recorded only as an unanchored diagnostic.

All parameters were restored exactly. No checkpoint was retained or promoted, and no
closed-loop hover test was run. The pass establishes only one-step local feasibility for
separating velocity response from transient height response; it authorizes the separately
bounded 25-attempt training protocol and is not evidence of learned damping by itself.
